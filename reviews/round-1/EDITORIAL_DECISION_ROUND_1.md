## Disagreements and Editorial Arbitration

### Disagreement 1: Must the release add new field evidence or narrow the application claim?

- **DA view**: A positive-
  \(K_i\) witness, broader uncertainty chamber, or semantically validated field case would be more persuasive; absent these, application claims must be narrowed to synthetic stress testing (`DA-adversarial.md`, lines 28–32 and 44–51).
- **R3 view**: The application defects are repairable without new field results if the event-closure, portfolio, uncertainty, and design gates are made explicit (`R3-applications.md`, lines 41–43 and 69–107).
- **EIC/R2 view**: The logistics sections are acceptable as a bounded protocol so long as operational value remains untested (`EIC-independent.md`, lines 96–104 and 168–170; `R2-domain.md`, lines 166–170).
- **Type**: Direction and severity disagreement.
- **Editor's resolution**: **No field study or new positive-cost witness is mandatory for this mathematical candidate release. Claim narrowing and protocol repair are mandatory.** If the author retains any empirical effectiveness or “real-world application result” claim, then new field evidence becomes required. Otherwise the release must describe a synthetic mathematical stress fixture and a prospective application research programme only.
- **Rationale**: This preserves the paper's mathematical contribution without allowing a proposed protocol to masquerade as application evidence; it follows the applications specialist's domain judgment and the DA's explicit narrowing alternative.

### Disagreement 2: Do the holding-box radii establish an intrinsic four-order robustness difference?

- **EIC/R1/R2 view**: The eight-vertex boxes are valid and useful local certificates when their limited scope is preserved (`EIC-independent.md`, lines 56–62; `R1-methodology.md`, lines 142–146; `R2-domain.md`, lines 65–67 and 159–164).
- **DA view**: The radii are constructed inner radii under one simultaneous relative \(H\)-norm and are not proved maximal; readers could mistake the four-order comparison for intrinsic operational robustness (`DA-adversarial.md`, lines 28–30).
- **R3 view**: The box varies only holding costs and needs a fixed/varied/omitted presentation before operational handoff (`R3-applications.md`, lines 89–97 and 142–146).
- **Type**: Interpretation/severity disagreement.
- **Editor's resolution**: **Retain the exact vertex result, but rename the quantities “certified inner radii under simultaneous relative holding-cost perturbation” and remove any implication of maximal or operational robustness.** Computing a maximal chamber is suggested, not required, if the narrower language is used.
- **Rationale**: The reviewers agree on the mathematical validity of the stated boxes; the dispute concerns what the radii mean. Precise naming resolves the overinterpretation without requiring unrelated new mathematics.

### Disagreement 3: Is an external clean-room result mandatory for this internal confirmation round?

- **R1/DA view**: Stronger independence should come from an unaffiliated implementation or mathematical audit, a separate minimal verifier, or proof-assistant formalisation (`R1-methodology.md`, lines 75–83 and 203–205; `DA-adversarial.md`, lines 26–27 and 50–51).
- **EIC/R2 view**: Specialist adjudication and re-review are required, while the release must preserve the fact that external assurance is absent (`EIC-independent.md`, lines 42–46 and 106–114; `R2-domain.md`, lines 41–43 and 234–236).
- **R3 view**: Defers this question to mathematical/software specialists (`R3-applications.md`, lines 18–24 and 37–43).
- **Type**: Assurance-threshold disagreement.
- **Editor's resolution**: **The confirmation review must verify the repaired proof and verifier, but model-mediated confirmation remains internal editorial review.** For an eventual explicitly unrefereed candidate, lack of unaffiliated reproduction may remain a visible `not-assessed` dimension only if all producer-side claims are labelled accordingly and no “independent” or external-peer-review status is asserted. Retaining unqualified “exact/certified” headline wording requires either an unaffiliated mathematical/clean-room audit or formal verification; otherwise the title, abstract, conclusion, and release metadata must consistently say “candidate exact oracle” and identify the finite-closure dependency.
- **Rationale**: This follows the strongest assurance concerns without falsely treating model-role review as external evidence. It also preserves the venue's ability to release an explicitly unrefereed candidate after internal repairs.

### Disagreement 4: Should the five-policy portfolio be retained as an operational candidate set?

- **EIC/R2 view**: The portfolio is a useful consequence of the active-envelope/fragility result when clearly bounded (`EIC-independent.md`, lines 56–62 and 164–166; `R2-domain.md`, lines 53–67).

For a fitted operational instance, regenerate an instance-specific deterministic candidate set under a preregistered materiality/regret tolerance and calendar feasibility; do not transfer the frozen five by default.

### Disagreement 5: How should the 42.6709% interval metric be treated?

## Decision Rationale

The five reports agree that the submission contains a worthwhile and unusually well-calibrated candidate contribution, so rejection would be disproportionate. They also agree that first-round acceptance is not supportable. The central exact-globality claim depends on a finite-generator, triple-tail, and termination chain that EIC, R1, R2, and the DA find insufficiently complete for a journal-readable theorem. R1 additionally identifies concrete release defects in path-dependent regenerated artifacts and certificate parsing, while R2 and R3 identify incorrect bibliographic records. These are not cosmetic: they affect globality, reproducibility, and source integrity.

The application material is valuable because it resists converting a synthetic relaxation gap into savings, but R3 and the DA identify two consequential transfer errors: a three-SKU group may not be closed under the common event, and the frozen five-policy portfolio is not an instance-specific operational set. These can be repaired by stricter semantics and claim narrowing; a field study is not required for a mathematical candidate release.

Major Revision, rather than Reject, is appropriate because no reviewer found a demonstrated arithmetic contradiction, the DA found no Critical defect, and each reviewer described a plausible repair path. Major Revision, rather than Minor Revision, is required because the changes affect the main theorem's proof presentation, the model definition, verifier trust boundary, literature lineage, and application handoff. Under Evidence Press rules this maps to `HOLD_FOR_REPAIR` until a point-by-point response, deterministic replay, and one confirmation review pass.

### Required Item Details

#### R1: Complete theorem, scope, and dependency chain

- **Problem**: The global optimum depends on a finite-generator/tail/totality chain that is compressed, partly external to the manuscript, and not fully quantified at cap-binding boundaries. The counting density, common-origin policy class, coefficient field, Γ supremum, and imported parent/Segev dependencies are not defined with journal-level precision (`EIC-independent.md`, lines 76–82; `R1-methodology.md`, lines 65–73 and 123–140; `R2-domain.md`, lines 83–109; `DA-adversarial.md`, lines 26 and 38).
- **Requirement**:
  1. Add formal definitions of counting density, feasible instances, policy phases/common origin, coefficient and cap domains, Γ, minima/infima, and admissibility of zero minor setup costs.
  2. State numbered finite-generation, pair-tail, triple-tail, totality, fixed-\(n\) monotonicity, and holding-box propositions with explicit hypotheses and dependency links.
  3. Supply a complete proof in the manuscript or an immutable citable appendix, including constants/quantifiers for approximation rates, cap-feasibility restoration, rational/irrational boundary cases, and strict/weak closing conditions.
  4. State exactly which claims are independent of the two-item predecessor and which import its candidate cap-reduction or endpoint result.
- **Acceptance criteria**:
  - A confirmation methodology/domain review can trace every headline consequence to a numbered result and complete proof.
  - No essential proof step is available only in mutable repository prose or code.
  - The common-origin fixed-interval scope and the conditional status of any broader resource interpretation are visible in the abstract and theorem statement.

#### R2: Verifier, schema, replay, and assurance repair

- **Problem**: The checker shares algebra/parsing with the producer solver; an empty or malformed frequency list can evade a length check; raw symbolic parsing is too permissive; mutation coverage is narrow; regenerated reports contain absolute paths and do not have a declared byte/semantic equivalence target (`R1-methodology.md`, lines 75–103 and 148–153; `DA-adversarial.md`, lines 27 and 40).
- **Requirement**:
  1. Add a versioned strict schema or equivalent validator with exact list lengths, types, required fields, uniqueness, safe expression grammar, and unknown-field policy.
  2. Remove unrestricted symbolic parsing of untrusted certificate text and add truncation, duplication, malformed-radical, omitted-record, unknown-field, and property/fuzz mutation tests.
  3. Serialize canonical repository-relative identifiers, add a fresh temporary-directory regression, and declare whether regeneration requires byte identity or semantic identity; verify that target after replay.
  4. Keep “solver-independent producer verifier” terminology and the shared-substrate limitation visible.
  5. Provide either an unaffiliated clean-room mathematical/software audit, proof-assistant formalisation, or—if neither exists—consistent “candidate exact” wording and `not-assessed` external assurance fields.
- **Acceptance criteria**:
