**Submission:** *An Exact Three-Item Independent-Cap Oracle and a Certified Multi-Item Relaxation-Gap Lower Bound*  
**Review role:** Devil's Advocate, first round  
**Review scope:** Frozen manuscript and frozen package evidence only  
**Conflict/independence statement:** No other reviewer report was consulted. This is an AI-simulated adversarial review, not unaffiliated human peer review or an independent mathematical reproduction.

The submission does several things well. It states an exact rational witness, separates the certified instance from the unresolved global constant, discloses same-producer verification, and refuses to convert a relaxation gap into a savings claim. The finite-closure architecture is also a substantive candidate contribution rather than a mere numerical search.

## Strongest Counter-Argument

The strongest case against publication in its present form is that the paper occupies an unstable middle ground: its mathematical claim is narrower and less independently secured than its framing suggests, while its logistics contribution is a proposed evaluation protocol rather than application evidence. The exact ratio is established only for a deliberately tuned, normalized, common-origin, three-item instance with zero item-specific setup costs and independent frequency caps. The global claim for that instance depends on a novel finite-generator and tail argument whose decisive proof is compressed in the manuscript, expanded only in a producer-authored package note, and replayed by code that independently duplicates some routines but shares the same provenance and exact-algebra substrate. Thus the many digits and 49,981-policy count can create a stronger impression of epistemic closure than the assurance chain warrants.

The operational extension does not repair this limitation. Its holding-cost boxes vary only three coefficients around synthetic points, and the reported radii are conservatively constructed inner boxes—not demonstrated maximal stability chambers. The five-policy portfolio is selected from the active-set construction; the frozen portfolio artifact records membership but does not itself certify exhaustive near-optimality across all policy classes, parameter drift, or omitted operational constraints. No representative logistics instances, calibrated cost mappings, shifted schedules, service models, or prospective data appear. Consequently, the work can support a promising candidate theorem and a useful software stress fixture, but it does not yet establish that the phenomenon is frequent, decision-relevant, or even preserved in a realistically parameterized neighborhood. The paper should be approved, if at all, as a rigorously bounded mathematical candidate with an application research agenda—not as a validated real-world logistics result.

## Ignored Alternative Explanations/Paths

1. **Engineered-degeneracy explanation:** The active-envelope family may primarily demonstrate that one can tune a low-dimensional synthetic model arbitrarily close to a policy crossover, rather than reveal a common structural pathology in logistics data. A distributional study over preregistered instance families would distinguish these narratives.
2. **Strictly positive-cost path:** A nearby instance with `Ki > 0` and a material full-dimensional robustness chamber would be more persuasive for applications than further decimal improvement at `Ki = 0`.
3. **Decision-quality path:** Compare exact cost, relaxation, incumbent periodic heuristics, and any Segev-style rounded policy. A weak lower bound does not imply a poor implementable policy; the operationally relevant question is whether decisions or costs change.
4. **Alternative policy-class path:** Allow or explicitly analyse phase-shifted calendars, non-common-origin periodic policies, or other operationally used replenishment classes before treating the common-origin oracle as a universal software benchmark.
5. **Upper-certificate path:** A universal three-item upper certificate, or even a parameter-region upper bound, would add more field knowledge now than additional tuning of the same lower-bound active family.
6. **Independent-specification path:** A clean mathematical specification plus unaffiliated implementation would test the theorem architecture more effectively than further mutation tests within the same code lineage.

## Unexamined Premise

The paper assumes that a large exact-to-relaxed ratio is a useful proxy for real decision risk. It is certainly a proof-analysis barrier and a software consistency test, but it does not follow that planners use this relaxation as an attainable plan, that relaxation error changes the chosen implementable policy, or that it predicts incumbent suboptimality. The strongest defensible “so what?” is therefore theoretical: the two-item obstruction does not extend and a particular lower-bound analysis cannot certify a smaller universal factor. The practical significance remains a hypothesis requiring decision-level evidence.

## Observations (Non-Defects)

- The exact arithmetic displayed for the headline witness is internally consistent across the manuscript, certificate, and replay artifacts inspected.
- The second-smallest-pair-complexity tail inequality appears directionally and algebraically coherent; I found no counterexample in the supplied bounded test or in this review's read-only reasoning audit.
- The monotonicity construction is logically plausible under the stated allowance `Ki = 0`, `Hi > 0`: deleting the appended item yields a feasible old schedule, while the relaxation extension adds a vanishing holding term.
- The primary Segev source does state a `5/(6 ln 2)` bound relative to the convex-relaxation optimum, so the manuscript is comparing compatible mathematical objects, subject to the source-status and endpoint caveats above.
- The manuscript's repeated refusal to infer savings, field benefit, independent reproduction, or the exact global constant is an important strength and should be preserved verbatim through revision.

## Recommendation

**MAJOR REVISION**

The exact rational lower-bound candidate is promising and appears worth preserving. Approval should wait for a journal-self-contained finite-closure proof, stronger independence in the assurance chain, correction or reframing of the interval-reduction metric, explicit relabelling of the robustness boxes as inner certificates, and a sharper separation between mathematical stress testing and real-world application evidence. The Evidence Press release gate should require a point-by-point response and a second-round verification review.

It is not 5/5 because this review is not a clean-room mathematical reproduction, did not formally verify the proof, and intentionally did not inspect other reviewers' reports.
