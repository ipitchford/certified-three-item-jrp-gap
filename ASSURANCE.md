The verifier is **solver-independent**, not independently produced: it does not import the discovery oracle, yet it was created in the same workflow and shares `ScaleCost`, exact comparison, rational conversion, and instance parsing. A successful replay demonstrates producer-side internal consistency, not theorem correctness or external reproduction. Verification reports use canonical repository-relative identifiers; canonical JSON has a byte-identity regeneration target.

## Claim ceiling

A producer-side unrefereed exact-computation candidate establishes the bundled three-item rational lower bound and, conditional on the candidate finite-closure argument, the declared fixed-item and holding-box consequences. It does not establish the exact global constant, a field benefit, causal impact, or any external assurance status.
