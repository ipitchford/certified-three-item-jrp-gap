# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to certified-three-item-jrp-gap 0.3.1-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22057665)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v0.3.1-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/certified-three-item-jrp-gap/>.

1. Corrected a percentage statement in §1, “Why the third item matters”, fourth paragraph. The archived text says the relaxation denominator is 14.8729146475% below the exact cost, or 12.9472771655% as a proportion of it. The 14.87% figure is the exact cost's excess over the relaxation. The sentence should read: “In a normalized synthetic instance, the exact cost exceeds the relaxation value by 14.8729146475%; equivalently, the relaxation is 12.9472771655% below the exact optimum.”

   Both numbers are correct; only the wording was reversed. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.

2. Corrected a formula label in the §1 table, row “preprint-upper separation”. The archived row gives the separation as (U − C*)/C* with U = 5/(6 ln 2). The printed value, 4.6588%, is (U − C*/L)/(C*/L), the separation measured against the ratio C*/L; (U − C*)/C* would be negative.

   The printed value is correct. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
